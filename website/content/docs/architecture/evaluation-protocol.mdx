Small eval sets and many candidate checkpoints make it easy to fool yourself. The protocol below exists to make that hard.

## Frozen suites

A suite is `evals/<version>/<name>/{manifest.json, *.jsonl}`. The manifest pins:

- dataset IDs and revisions,
- the base-model tokenizers the suite was admitted against (records must encode without truncation under all of them),
- the sha256 of every partition.

`load_split` verifies hashes on load; large partitions are mirrored on the Hub dataset `jaredpalmer/kev-suites` at a pinned revision. **Frozen files are never modified**; new data is a new version.

| Suite | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `decision-v7` | Training recipe; `development` for in-distribution selection, `calibration` held out |
| `transfer-v4` | Out-of-domain: six never-trained sources + held-out rule structures; the headline "new sources" numbers |
| `transfer-v9` | Adds MMLU-Pro, buried states (long-context), and unknowable items |
| `external/*` | Third-party items with published Jev numbers: SemIf, scienthoon, WANLI, TypeSafe public, ekzhang MMLU-Pro |

## Partitions and the locked test

- **development** selects models, fits temperatures, and tunes thresholds.
- **test** is locked. `kev.benchmark` refuses to read it without `--allow-test`, and each candidate reads it **at most once**. Locked reads live in `runs/locked/`.
- Temperatures are fitted on in-distribution development rows only, never on transfer or test.

Jev was scored on development items only, so Jev comparisons are against Kev's development numbers.

## Pre-registration

Each research round in [`PLAN.md`](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev/blob/main/PLAN.md) states candidates, metrics and adopt-if gates **before** training or reading results, and records negatives with the same prominence as wins. Example gate (round 4, rotation averaging): "becomes a serving option if transfer accuracy improves ≥ +0.5 pp with a paired CI lower bound ≥ 0, or the permutation flip rate halves." Registrations that turn out to be ill-posed are corrected in writing, not silently.

## Statistics

- **Paired, record-clustered bootstrap.** `kev.compare --candidate A --reference B` resamples records (not questions, since questions within a record are correlated) and reports the paired difference with a 95% interval. The default is 1,000 resamples; release confirmations have used 2,000.
- **Incumbent rule.** In automated search, a challenger replaces the champion only if its paired per-task macro delta on transfer accuracy has a 95% lower bound ≥ −1 pp and a positive point estimate. At 0.6B the interval is about ±5 pp wide, so small "improvements" don't displace a champion.
- **Resolution matters.** On ~650 transfer questions, coverage at a 5% error budget has intervals tens of points wide; round-4 findings note that it can't resolve ±5 pp. Brier and confident-error rate are more stable and increasingly the registered metrics.

## Metrics reported per run

`kev.benchmark` writes `rows.json` (per question, with raw logits and the inference temperature) and `report.json`:

- accuracy, NLL, multi-class Brier, 10-bin ECE on $$p_{\max}$$, confident-error rate at $$p_{\max} \ge 0.9$$;
- selective prediction: risk–coverage curve, AURC, coverage at an error budget (in-sample maximum);
- per-task and per-source breakdowns;
- permutation flip rate and mean max $$\Delta p$$ on permuted Choice items;
- question-isolation deltas;
- an unknowable report (how often the model is confident on questions the state can't answer).

Because raw logits are saved, any temperature can be applied after the fact (`kev.calibrate --rows`).

## Claims are traceable

Published README and model-card numbers are listed in `docs/claims.json` with the report each comes from. `scripts/verify_claims.py` runs in CI (`tests/test_conventions.py`) and fails if a published number doesn't match a committed report. Every `result.json` carries the suite hash, code hashes and git commit.

## Known weaknesses of the protocol

- One seed per configuration for most comparisons; three seeds of one earlier 4B recipe (Qwen3 generation) scored 0.773 / 0.790 / 0.770 on transfer, a 2-point spread.
- External gates in round 5 were point estimates on 89–256 questions.
- The transfer suite is 764 development records; per-source cells have tens of items.
- Jev is a moving target scored at one point in time.
